Remus Variable is a variable sans-serif derived from TeX Gyre Heros, which is based on URW Nimbus Sans L — the free equivalent of Helvetica. The styles of TeX Gyre Heros (regular, bold, condensed, italic) are combined into a single variable font with three axes: weight, width, and italic.

Text
TeX Gyre Heros is based on URW Nimbus Sans L, kindly released by URW++ Design and Development Inc. under GFL. The Vietnamese glyphs were added by Han The Thanh. Remus Variable combines the styles of TeX Gyre Heros into a single file with axes for weight, width, and italic.
URW Nimbus Sans L is based on Helvetica, designed by Max Miedinger with Eduard Hoffmann at the Haas Type Foundry in 1957. Helvetica became one of the most widely used sans-serif typefaces in the world, valued for its neutrality and breadth of weights.
